Built out of pure frustration.

I just wanted to loop a 5-second solo section on a YouTube video and gradually build up the speed without taking my hands off the fretboard every ten seconds.

Existing tools were either too clunky, bloated, or locked behind expensive monthly subscriptions. I built RiffQ to be the exact practice workstation I needed for my own playing.

Whether I'm tackling fast riffs and solo sections that I'm struggling with, gradually building them up to full speed, looping specific parts of a solo, or just looping entire songs from start to finish as I learn them, RiffQ gets out of the way. It automates the annoying parts of practice—managing loop transitions, drill queues, and timing your rest gaps—so you can focus entirely on the reps.
